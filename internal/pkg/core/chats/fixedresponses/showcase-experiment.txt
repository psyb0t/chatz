The onboarding_v2 variant won, and it's statistically significant. Conversion rose from 22.4% to 27.1% — a 21% relative lift at p=0.008 across 48,200 sessions. Just as important, activation got faster: median time-to-activate dropped from 11 minutes on control to 7 on the variant.
